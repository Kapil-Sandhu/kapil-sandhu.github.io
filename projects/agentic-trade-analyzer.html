🎯 1. Problem Statement & Architectural Objectives

1. Inefficiency of Chronological Time Bars

Standard time-based candles (1m, 5m, 1h) oversample during quiet market consolidation (generating high-frequency noise) and critically undersample during fast flash crashes, hiding vital order flow shifts when volatility explodes.

2. Fragility of Single-Model LLM Decisions

Relying on a single prompt or standalone agent to execute live trades induces catastrophic hallucinations, confirmation bias, and mathematical blindness, making the system vulnerable to institutional market traps.

3. Macro Reasoning vs Micro Execution Gap

LLM cognitive reasoning requires seconds, whereas microsecond order-book sweeps occur in milliseconds. Delegating real-time order routing directly to an LLM results in extreme slippage and unmanageable latency.

4. Static Risk vs Fat-Tailed Tail Risk

Traditional fixed-percentage stop losses fail in cryptocurrency markets, which exhibit extreme non-Gaussian kurtosis and volatility clustering. Positions must be protected by statistical Expected Shortfall (CVaR).

💡
The Solution: A decoupled hybrid quant-AI engine that partitions work into three distinct execution paths: a 10ms Fast Risk Poller, a 5s Continuous Math Engine, and a 2m Cognitive AI Mixture-of-Experts Brain.

🏗️ 2. Decoupled 5-Pillar Microservice Architecture

⚡ Pillar 1: Data Ingestion & Dual-Clock Microstructure Layer

nervous_system.py & bootstrapper.py
  • Information-Driven Sampling ($1M Dollar Bars): Replaces chronological clock time with volume-driven sampling, cutting an informational slice every accumulated $1,000,000 USD of volume with fractional tick boundary splitting.
  • O(1) Streaming Memory EWMA: Calculates running mean and variance on tick streams without storing unbounded historical arrays, tracking fast ($\alpha=0.2$) and slow ($\alpha=0.02$) Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) in real time.
  • Divergence Z-Scores & Warmup: Emits real-time breakout signals (BULLISH_BREAKOUT, BEARISH_BREAKDOWN). Bootstrapper pre-fills Redis cache from Binance klines before live feed activation.

📊 Pillar 2: Multi-Timeframe Technical & Structural Analysis

ta_engine.py
  • Vectorized Indicators across 6 Timeframes: Computes momentum (RSI-14, MACD 12/26/9), trend envelopes (SMAs & EMAs 10–200), and volatility (ATR-14, Bollinger Bands 20, 2.0) across 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, and synthetic Dollar Bars.
  • Key Structural Support & Resistance (KRS Zones): Applies centered rolling windows ($\pm 15$ bars) to identify pivot highs/lows and merges neighboring liquidity clusters within a 0.1% price tolerance.

📝 Pillar 3: Context Formulation & Semantic Compression

context_builder.py
  • Strict Pydantic AI Schemas: Enforces strong type validation on TrendAverages, TechnicalState, and MarketSummary to prevent malformed prompt injections.
  • Semantic Regime Translation: Converts floating-point values into descriptive structural tokens (e.g., "UPTREND OVEREXTENDED", "UPTREND PULLBACK", "CONSOLIDATION") to prevent LLM numerical confusion.
  • Prompt Optimization: Rounds numeric metrics to 2 decimal places to minimize token overhead and latency.

🧠 Pillar 4: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Adversarial Reasoning & Dual FSM

ai_orchestrator.py
Pipeline A: Alpha Hunter (Trade Entry)
  • Technical Analyst Agent: Synthesizes multi-timeframe micro/macro market data.
  • 3-Round Debate: Bull Quant vs Bear Quant engage in structured opening, cross-examination rebuttals, and closing predictions.
  • Master Portfolio Manager: Evaluates transcript in a <scratchpad>, issuing direction, Conviction Score (0.0–1.0), and a Semantic Invalidation Condition (e.g., "1H CVD breakdown & 15m RSI < 30").
Pipeline B: Thesis Validator (Trade Audit)
  • Position Manager vs Risk Auditor: Actively defends vs attacks the live thesis against real-time order flow decay.
  • Semantic Exit Actions: Emits HOLD, REDUCE_EXPOSURE, or ABORT without waiting for stop loss cascades.
  • Self-Distillation Logging: Logs all debate reasoning to distillation_dataset.jsonl for offline Small Language Model (SLM) fine-tuning.

🛡️ Pillar 5: Institutional Risk Management & Deterministic Execution

order_router.py

Operating on a 10ms high-frequency polling loop, ensuring deterministic quantitative risk strictly overrides and constrains AI recommendations:

Pre-Trade Risk Gates
  • Global Circuit Breaker: Halts all trading if drawdown exceeds 5% from peak equity.
  • 99% Expected Shortfall (CVaR): Analyzes worst 1% left-tail returns over 300 Dollar Bars. Suppresses trade if ES₉₉ > 5%.
  • Fractional Quarter-Kelly: Sizes positions based on Kelly fraction scaled by AI Conviction and ATR volatility.
4-Tier Dynamic In-Position FSM
  • Tier 1A (Floor): Hard Stop Loss at 1.5x ATR (non-negotiable).
  • Tier 1B (The Banker): +2.0x ATR profit executes 50% trim & moves SL to Breakeven.
  • Tier 2A (Toxic Flow): Immediate exit on VPIN/sweep against trade.
  • Tier 2B (Choke): GMM shift to CHAOTIC tightens SL to 0.5x ATR & trims 50%.
  • Tier 2C (Decay): 50 Dollar Bars without target hit triggers 50% trim.

📊 3. Mathematical Formulations & Statistical Invariants

• Lee-Ready Tick Rule & CVD:
$$d_t = \begin{cases} +1 & \text{if } p_t > p_{t-1} \\ -1 & \text{if } p_t < p_{t-1} \\ d_{t-1} & \text{if } p_t = p_{t-1} \end{cases} \quad \implies \quad \text{CVD}_T = \sum_{t=1}^T d_t \cdot V_t$$

Classifies aggressor direction on every Binance WebSocket trade, integrating cumulative delta over continuous volume bars.

• 99% Expected Shortfall (CVaR Tail Risk):
$$\text{CVaR}_{\alpha}(X) = \mathbb{E}\left[ X \mid X \le \text{VaR}_{\alpha}(X) \right] = \frac{1}{\alpha}\int_{0}^{\alpha} \text{VaR}_u(X)\,du, \quad \alpha=0.01$$

Evaluates the average loss in the worst 1% of statistical outcomes over rolling 300 dollar bars before permitting order entry.

• Fractional Kelly Position Sizing:
$$f^* = \max\left(0, p - \frac{1-p}{b}\right) \times \frac{1}{4} \times \text{Conviction}, \quad \text{Size} = \frac{\text{Equity} \times f^*}{1.5 \times \text{ATR}_\%}$$

Scales risk capital proportional to AI conviction and inversely to current 14-period ATR market volatility.

• O(1) Memory Streaming Variance:
$$\mu_t = (1-\alpha)\mu_{t-1} + \alpha x_t, \quad \sigma_t^2 = (1-\alpha)\sigma_{t-1}^2 + \alpha(x_t - \mu_t)^2$$

Maintains real-time running mean and volatility on millions of trades without unbounded memory allocations.
