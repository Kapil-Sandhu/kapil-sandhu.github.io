CPro: Multi-Scale Momentum Transformer & CPro 2.0

Bitcoin Short-Term Direction & Volatility-Conditioned Momentum Forecasting Pipeline

An institutional-grade quantitative machine learning research framework engineered to forecast high-probability Bitcoin (BTC/USD) momentum directions (Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral/Skip) and continuous momentum strength. Built and validated on over 5 years (2020–2025) of 1-minute tick data (~3.15 million bars), the project resolves financial non-stationarity, regime collapse, and lookahead data leakage through event-driven CUSUM sampling, 4-state Gaussian HMM gating, and cross-timeframe RoPE attention.

2. Quantitative Methodology & Architectural Pillars

PILLAR 01 12.5% Sampling Density

Symmetric CUSUM Event Filter

Standard chronological 1-minute bars oversample during quiet market consolidation and miss fast volatility bursts. BTC-Cpro employs a Symmetric Cumulative Sum (CUSUM) filter that continuously tracks positive and negative price return deviations.

• Event Trigger: Emits a discrete sample only when cumulative price drift exceeds a volatility-adjusted dynamic threshold.
• Noise Filter: Resets accumulation buffers upon trigger, discarding sideways random walk ticks and isolating directional momentum.
Reduced 2.63M clean 1-min bars into 329,602 discrete events with maximal information density.
PILLAR 02 Triple-Barrier

Triple-Barrier Execution Targets

Replaces fixed-time horizons with dynamic, volatility-adjusted profit and loss boundaries calculated from live 14-period Average True Range (ATR):

• Bullish (2): Upper take-profit barrier hit first (+1.5x ATR) within a 30-bar forward window.
• Bearish (0): Lower stop-loss barrier hit first (-1.5x ATR), marking downside breakdown.
• Neutral (1): Vertical 30-minute timeout reached before either barrier is touched (Skip low-conviction chop).
Generates continuous strength score based on 95th-percentile capped normalized forward return.
PILLAR 03 11 Dynamic Features

Stationary Order Flow & Intra-Candles

Converts non-stationary raw price ticks into strictly bounded, stationary multi-timeframe feature tensors (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h) with 11 causal intra-bar metrics:

• Order Flow: Signed Dollar Volume Imbalance ratio tracking aggressive buyer vs seller pressure.
• Whale Activity: Average trade size intensity metric detecting institutional order book sweeps.
• Candle Metrics: Real-time completion progress, upper/lower wick rejection ratios, and body-to-range proportions.
Guarantees strict causality for partially-formed higher-timeframe bars.
PILLAR 04 Sigmoid Gate

4-State Gaussian HMM Gating

An unsupervised Hidden Markov Model detects macro market regimes using smoothed return momentum, realized volatility, and price velocity:

• Regime Gating: Modulates intermediate Transformer activations through a learned Sigmoid gating vector conditioned on HMM state probabilities.
• Adaptive Filter: Suppresses signals during State 0 (ranging chop, 33.6% of data) while amplifying conviction during trending regimes.
Prevents false-breakout trading during sideways low-volatility churn.
PILLAR 05 RoPE Attention

Multi-Scale Patch Transformer (MSMT)

Applies Reversible Instance Normalization (RevIN), patches 4-bar time slices, and encodes relative temporal distances:

• Rotary Embedding: Rotates query and key vectors in complex embedding space so attention weights naturally decay with temporal distance.
• Multi-Timeframe: Allows fine-grained 1-minute execution tokens to cross-attend into macro 5m, 15m, and 1h trend context simultaneously.
Cross-timeframe attention preserves macro trend direction without latency penalty.
PILLAR 06 L-BFGS Calibrated

Leakage Control & Temperature Scaling

Guarantees strict out-of-sample statistical integrity and empirical probability calibration across five years of historical data:

• Purged Embargo CV: Inserts a 200-bar embargo buffer between training and test splits to completely eliminate auto-regressive lookahead leakage.
• Probability Scaling: Uses post-hoc L-BFGS temperature calibration ($T^* = 0.9243$), ensuring predicted confidence accurately matches empirical win rates.
Guarantees probability calibration so that predicted confidence reflects true win probability.

5. Software Modules & Execution Pipeline

Stage 01: Data Ingestion & QC clean_resample.py

Loads 3.15M raw 1-minute OHLCV tick records from 2020–2025, checks timestamp continuity, imputes micro-gaps, and computes 5m, 15m, and 1h resampled aggregations.

Stage 02: CUSUM & Triple Barrier cusum_labeler.py

Executes the Symmetric CUSUM volatility filter down to 329,602 discrete trading events, dynamically calculating $1.5\times$ ATR profit/stop boundaries and 30-bar vertical timeouts.

Stage 03: Feature Engineering & HMM features_hmm.py

Constructs 16 stationary technical and order flow indicators per timeframe alongside 11 causal intra-candle features, fitting a 4-state Gaussian HMM with boosted transition persistence.

Stage 04: MSMT Training & Calibration msmt_trainer.py

Trains the Multi-Scale Momentum Transformer via Lion optimizer and progressive multi-task loss schedule, executing post-hoc L-BFGS temperature scaling on validation logits ($T^* = 0.9243$).
